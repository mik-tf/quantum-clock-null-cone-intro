# Does a quantum clock still obey the bound?

Author: Michaël Perreault

An introduction, in plain language, to the research paper *A one-mode quantization of the clock bound on a null cone, and its sensitivity to operator ordering* (concept DOI [10.5281/zenodo.24575468](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24575468)). The paper has the proofs. This note has the idea, and it says what the paper does and does not claim.
